# Why

Why am I writing this book? 

Because my sons will ask me, and one day their sons: Dad, Grandpa, what were you doing during the Great American Revolution? 

Well, Sons, Grandsons, first, I tried (and failed) to audit Minnesota elections; and then, I was writing this book. The media was not going to tell this story, not this way. Nor were the influencers connected to them, all proxies for the intelligence agencies, themselves orchestrating the 'politics' that are the entertainment division for the military industrial complex. Nor locally the tax-payer funded Minnesota Historical Society, established more than nine years prior to Minnesota becoming a state in the union, with the Minnesota Territory legislature's fifth act, with an interesting purpose: "...and the object of said Society shall be the collection and preservation of a Library, Mineralogical and Geological specimens, Indian curiosities and other matters and things connected with, and **calculated to illustrate and perpetuate the history** [emphasis added] and settlement of said Territory."

https://www.revisor.mn.gov/laws/1849/0/General+Laws/Chapter/44/pdf/

Included in the latter organization's executive council today are ex officio members: the current governor, lieutenant governor, secretary of state, state auditor, and attorney general. One can imagine their view of the recent history. The people, meanwhile, must endeavor to record our version, to illustrate and perpetuate what we have observed for ourselves and how we have participated. Historians can later compare accounts to learn and determine what they may. Likely, this era is to be carefully studied. If we succeed in preserving the essence, then unlike so many previous eras, where tangible evidence is scarce, there will be something for the historians and anthropologists and anyone else monitoring Earth, from near range or from afar, to go on.

This book will be continuously updated on Leanpub, or a similar service, until the time when Minnesota succeeds in conducting an election where every voter wins. That is, an actual, real, election, as defined in part by the foundations this guide lays out. At present,in every election, every voter loses because we cannot check the work of our administrators nor the electronic programming nor any access to those electronics, authorized or unauthorized. This book begins a discussion that asks, How can every voter win? Every voter wins when we can meaningfully audit every part of the election process, that it is reconstructable, that it can be shown substantively that the inputs equal the outputs. 

Meaningful elections can only occur, though, when the people are ready to reclaim their elections. To say another way, it is a prerequisite that we, collectively, desire to do this work, to first raise awareness about how our infrastructure is not fit for purpose and then to accept the task of building an alternative. 

Therefore, this book will serve, until that time and until the related conditions are met (such as lawful accountability for wrongdoing), as a resource and a reference and a starting point, for experts and election workers and ordinary citizens doing extraordinary work to create the change needed. If we succeed, we will have taken an important step toward self governance.
