# Are You Ready?

At the close of the third day of the Cyber Symposium, in 2021, soon after I'd spoken with Col. Waldren about calling for new elections, Mark Cook walked by. After a brief conversation, Mark asked: "Are you ready?" 

I said, Yes. 

But what was I ready for?

Where we are, especially now, I think it's important to be ready for anything. To open one's mind to possibilities not previously examined. 

The fact that elections have been designed to be controlled will be perhaps not be the most surprising thing we collectively learn in the immediately coming years. However, that is where this book will focus. 

So, as Mark asked me, I now ask you, reader and citizen auditor... Are you ready?
