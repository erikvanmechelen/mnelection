# Why

Why am I writing this book? 

Because my sons will ask me, and one day their sons: Dad, what were you doing during the Great American Revolution? 

Well, Sons, Grandsons, I was writing this book. The media was not going to tell this story. Nor were the influencers connected to them, all proxies for the intelligence agencies, themselves orchestrating the 'politics' that are the entertainment division for the military industrial complex. Nor locally the tax-payer funded Minnesota Historical Society, established more than nine years prior to Minnesota becoming a state in the union, with the Minnesota Territory legislature's fifth act, with an interesting purpose: "...and the object of said Society shall be the collection and preservation of a Library, Mineralogical and Geological specimens, Indian curiosities and other matters and things connected with, and **calculated to illustrate and perpetuate the history** [emphasis added] and settlement of said Territory."

https://www.revisor.mn.gov/laws/1849/0/General+Laws/Chapter/44/pdf/

Included in the latter organization's executive council are current ex officio members: the current governor, lieutenant governor, secretary of state, state auditor, and attorney general. One can imagine their view of the recent history. The people, meanwhile, must endeavor to record our version, to illustrate and perpetuate what we have observed for ourselves and how we have participated. Historians can later compare accounts to learn what they may. Likely, this era is to be carefully studied.

This book will be continuously updated on Leanpub, or a similar service, until the time when Minnesota succeeds in conducting an actual, real, election, as defined by the foundations this guide lays out, an election where instead of every voter losing (because we cannot check the work of our admins nor the electronics), every voter wins because we can meaningfully audit every part of the process. 

Meaningful elections can only occur when the people are ready to reclaim their elections. Therefore, this book will serve, until that time, as a resource and a reference and a starting point, for experts and election workers and ordinary citizens doing extraordinary work to both raise awareness and create the change needed. If we succeed, we will have taken an important step toward self governance.
